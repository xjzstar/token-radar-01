# Screenshots

Three screenshots belong in screenshots/, taken from the real dashboard:

1. `1-board.png` — the board
2. `2-detail.png` — a protocol's detail view
3. `3-filtered.png` — a filtered view

Each must be a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 512 KiB, long side at most 1440px and short side at least 800px. Taken with headless Chromium (Playwright) from the real dashboard at 1440x900: 1-board.png (169 KiB), 2-detail.png (230 KiB, Steakhouse Financial), 3-filtered.png (162 KiB, RWA category). The capture script lives in ~/workspace/makefun/shot-tool/shot.mjs. Do not substitute generated or stock images.
